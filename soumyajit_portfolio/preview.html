The story behind the numbers

It started with home visits in Kolkata. Early sales taught me to listen first: the objection people say out loud is often only the beginning of the real conversation. That principle has followed me across every role—from individual selling to managing revenue and teams.

My career has moved across very different sales environments: family-focused education, hotel partner acquisition, institutional partnerships, high-ticket career programs and an international AI/ML business. The common thread is turning messy customer and business problems into clear decisions, then building the habits and systems that make results repeatable.

Problems I solved—in the real world

Detailed problem, diagnosis, approach, outcome and learning. Verified outcomes are distinguished from estimates or context.

Toppr

Problem

Parents hesitated on a long-term learning plan because timing, relevance and value needed to be understood—not just explained.

Diagnosis

A family had different academic timelines for two children, so a generic product pitch would not resolve their real concern.

Approach

Asked about each child's stage and future requirements, then mapped a plan around family priorities rather than repeating product features.

Outcome

Closed a learning plan worth approximately ₹2.5 lakh—the documented best deal in the portfolio source.

Learning

Diagnose before persuading. Surface objections are often prompts to ask better questions.

OYO

Problem

Hotel owners evaluated partnership economics, operating realities, pricing and trust—not brand promises alone.

Diagnosis

Acquisition required territory understanding, commercial negotiation and confidence in onboarding and ongoing partner operations.

Approach

Worked directly with owners on prospecting, commercial terms and onboarding; coordinated with Revenue, Operations and Quality to support occupancy and retention.

Outcome

Onboarded 500+ rooms across West Bengal and Andaman & Nicobar Islands. Portfolio estimates are not personal revenue.

Learning

Partnerships work when the commercial model is credible and operating support is clear.

Vedantu

Accredian

Problem

High-ticket executive programs require career ROI clarity and a sales vertical that can sustain predictable performance.

Diagnosis

Buyers were experienced professionals with objections around ROI, timelines and alternatives. Team performance required coaching beyond activity counts.

Approach

Used consultative discovery, coached junior sellers, maintained CRM/pipeline hygiene, and later led 6–7 consultants while continuing to sell.

Outcome

Reported ₹11–14 lakh/month personal revenue as Principal Consultant; later team vertical reported at ₹65–70 lakh/month. Built partnerships and corporate training.

Learning

Management requires personal sales credibility plus an operating cadence.

Scaler

Problem

Performance needed to scale beyond personal closing; discovery quality and pipeline movement were key levers.

Diagnosis

Activity alone wasn't enough. Deals were lost in discovery and the team needed stage conversion visibility, hygiene and coaching.

Approach

Improved consultative discovery as an IC. After promotion, ran weekly 1:1s, pipeline reviews, live call coaching, conversion tracking and cross-functional feedback loops.

Outcome

Reported ₹30–40 lakh/month IC revenue. After promotion, team revenue reported at ₹75–80 lakh/month. Senior IC achieved 8–9 closures/month vs 4–5 target for three consecutive quarters; Q1 2026 award.

Learning

Conversion quality is coachable; leadership leverage comes from improving repeatable team decisions.
